DarkRush: Survivors privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Pablo Campos Rojas ("we"), reachable at sparkhold.game@gmail.com, is responsible for the data that DarkRush: Survivors ("the game") processes.

In short

  • We don't ask for your name, email, phone number or contacts to play.
  • If you want, you can save your account with an email and a password, and pick a player name that your friends and your clan will see.
  • The messages you write in a clan's chat are seen by that clan's members.
  • Your progress is saved on your phone, with a copy on our server linked to an anonymous ID.
  • If you buy something, we keep the store receipt so we can deliver it.
  • The game shows no ads from other companies. If it ever adds them, this policy will say so first, and they will only be personalized if you are 16 or older and you turn that on yourself.
  • You can delete your account and your data in the game: Settings → Delete my account.

We don't use analytics tools and we don't sell your data.

Who else processes it

  • Supabase (servers in Ireland, European Union): hosts the database and the anonymous accounts. It acts as our processor.
  • Apple and Google: process payments. We never receive your card details.

If any of them is outside the European Economic Area, the transfer relies on the standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission.

How long we keep it

  • For as long as you have the account.
  • Anonymous accounts with no activity for 180 days and no purchases are deleted automatically.
  • Deleting your account removes your progress, your entitlements and your account data. Purchase records are kept, no longer linked to you, for as long as accounting and tax law requires (six years, as Spain's Commercial Code requires).

Children

The game is meant for players aged 13 and over. Players under 16 never see personalized ads, whatever the settings say.

Your rights

You can access, correct, delete, object to or restrict the processing of your data, and take a copy with you. Write to sparkhold.game@gmail.com. Deletion is also available in the game. If you're not happy with our answer, you can complain to your data protection authority (in Spain, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos, www.aepd.es; elsewhere, the one in your country).

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that affects you, the game will show it to you before you keep playing.
