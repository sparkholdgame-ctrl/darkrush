Delete your DarkRush: Survivors account

Last updated: 21 November 2026

You can delete your DarkRush: Survivors account, and the data that goes with it, at any time.

In the game

Open Settings → Delete my account and confirm. The account is deleted straight away.

Without the game

If the game is no longer on your phone, write to sparkhold.game@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account". Tell us your player name and, if you saved your account, the email you used. We delete the account within 30 days and write back to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your progress: chapters, gear, heroes, gold, gems, upgrades and missions.
  • What you bought inside the game.
  • Your account data: the anonymous ID, the email and password if you saved them, your player name and your public card.
  • Your friends list, your place in a clan and the problem reports you sent.

What is kept

  • Messages you already wrote in a clan's chat stay in that chat, shown with the player name you had, but no longer linked to your account. If you want them removed too, say so in your email.
  • Purchase records, no longer linked to you, for as long as accounting and tax law requires (six years, as Spain's Commercial Code requires).

Anonymous accounts with no activity for 180 days and no purchases are deleted automatically.
