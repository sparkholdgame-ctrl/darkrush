How it plays

1

Move with one thumb

Your character fires on its own. You just steer, dodge and pick up the sparks enemies leave behind.

2

Choose 1 of 3

Every level-up offers three skills. Pair a weapon with the right passive and it evolves into something much bigger.

3

Come back stronger

Gold and gear carry over. Upgrade, merge equipment and take on the next chapter.

Why it's different

10 heroes, 300 chapters

Unlock heroes with their own abilities and fight through 29 worlds, each with its own creatures and bosses, plus 4 game modes with prizes of their own. Energy refills by itself.

No ads that interrupt you

Nothing pops up between runs. A reward is always your choice, and chests show their odds as they are right now.

Short runs, real choices

A run takes a few minutes. Every pick matters, and the boss gets angrier if you stall.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. There are optional purchases: gems, the season pass and some heroes.

Do I need an account?

No. Your progress is saved on your phone and backed up anonymously. You can delete it from Settings at any time.

Are there ads?

No ads pop up on their own. If the game offers a reward for watching one, it is always your choice.

Need help?

Write to sparkhold.game@gmail.com and we will answer you. You can also report a problem from the game: Settings, then Report a problem.

Which phones?

iPhone (iOS 14 or later) and Android.

Coming soon

DarkRush: Survivors is in testing. It will be free on the App Store and Google Play.
